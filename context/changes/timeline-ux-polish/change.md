# Timeline UX Polish

Slice S-05 from `context/foundation/roadmap.md`. Adds visual polish to the day-by-day timeline: a colored intensity bar per day (Low/Medium/High), a "Keep Together" toggle to force all attractions to stay on one day, and replaces the text-based tight schedule warning with the new intensity system.

**Outcome:** User sees at a glance how full each day is, and can force a day to keep all attractions together even if it means overstuffing.
